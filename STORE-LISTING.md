See what your agents are working on, what needs you, and what comes next—all in
one KiroCrew overview. Follow recorded progress across sessions, models, and
providers without opening every conversation to piece the work together. When
agents work together, compare their recorded updates and next actions side by
side, follow handoffs, and open the conversation where your input matters.

The manifest's five use cases describe situations and outcomes, following the
revised guidance in [issue #12294](https://github.com/kirodotdev/KiroCrew/issues/12294#issuecomment-5750170310).
The [walkthrough](docs/first-overview.md) shows how to build a useful overview
with two sessions and a recorded review handoff.

The app includes a Python backend and requires app-specific execution trust.
It uses local app storage and the configured gateway; no external analytics or
telemetry destination is configured. The gallery uses fictional demonstration
data rather than private session records.

Submit with KiroCrew's App Store listing issue template and the exact release
commit. The catalog maintainer reviews and publishes the listing.
